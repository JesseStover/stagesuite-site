Privacy policy

StageSuite and 2ndRodeo · last updated 20 October 2026

StageSuite (stage plots and show management) and 2ndRodeo (the crew's phone app) are made by one independent developer. This page says what the apps keep, where, and why. In short: your plots and shows stay on your device unless you choose to put a show in the cloud; nothing is sold, and there are no ads.

What stays on your device

  • Your plots, shows, crew lists, gear lists and settings are saved as files on your device (in Download/StageSuite).
  • The camera is used only to scan QR codes; photos of input lists are read on the device. Neither is sent anywhere by the app.
  • Crew live (sharing a show over the venue's Wi-Fi) goes directly between the devices on that network, not through the internet.

If you sign in and use the cloud (StageSuite)

  • Google sign-in: your Google account's name, email address and an account id, so the cloud knows which shows are yours.
  • Shows you put in the cloud: the show and its plots, including what you typed into them — crew names, phone numbers and email addresses, call times, acts and their gear. Only you and the people you invite (by their email, with the jobs you give them) can open it. You can stop sharing or remove people at any time.
  • Crew bookings: when you book crew, the booking (show name, days, call times, the person's name and section) is kept in the cloud so they can answer. Anyone with the booking's link can read it and answer it, so the link is only sent to the person booked.
  • Notifications: a device id from Google's messaging service, so answers to your bookings can reach you.
  • Plots shared by a cloud QR: when a plot is too big to print as a QR, a copy goes to the cloud and the printed QR opens it.

2ndRodeo (crew)

  • No account is needed. A random id stays on the phone so the engineer can tell your answers come from the same phone.
  • When you open a booking link, your answers (I'm in / Can't make it, and an optional note) go to the engineer who booked you, and the phone's messaging id is added to that booking so changes to it reach you.
  • The name and phone number you enter are sent to the engineer's tablet when you join a show.

Crash reports and feedback

  • Crash reports (what went wrong, the app version and the device model) go to Google Firebase Crashlytics to fix problems.
  • If you use Send feedback, your note, a screenshot of the screen, the app's problem log, the build number and the device model are sent; you choose whether to add the show file.

Who processes the data

The cloud parts run on Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Cloud Functions), in the United States. Google processes this data on the developer's behalf under its own terms and privacy policy.

Purchases

Any purchase is handled by Google Play; the app only learns which products your account owns.

Keeping and deleting

  • Files on your device are yours to keep or delete.
  • A show in the cloud stays until you remove it from the cloud; bookings stay with the show's owner.
  • To have your cloud data and account record deleted, email the address below; it is done within 30 days.

Children

The apps are for people working in live music and are not aimed at children under 13.
