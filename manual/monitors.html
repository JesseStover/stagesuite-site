Monitors & RF

This page is switched off for now: the Monitors & RF page is hidden in the app. The choices below that live in a band's Setup → Monitors (Wedges, House IEMs, Their IEMs, Their rack, our mixes) still work.

Each mix — wedges, in-ears, side fills — and where it leaves the desk (desk outputs, a stage box's returns, or both: ⋮ → Feeds & cue), then the amp channel or transmitter it feeds. Pick House or an act at the top; Number the mixes numbers the house's.

  • Amp racks: passive wedges on amp channels; powered ones take the line.
  • In-ears: transmitters and their packs; a stereo mix takes two buses.
  • Simple | Detailed shows the mixes as a patch bay; Auto-cable cables the amp racks and in-ear transmitters.
  • RF batteries shows packs that are low or not heard.
  • Monitors sheet prints it for the monitor engineer.
