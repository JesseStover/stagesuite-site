PatchWork Live on Windows

The same app on a Windows laptop or desktop, for the desk work — building the house, the master patch and plot, the running order and the bands' plots — beside the tablet you take to the stage. A show moves between the two as a file.

Installing

There is no installer yet. You get a folder (usually a zip — unzip it anywhere you like, for example your Documents or Desktop). Open it and double-click PatchWorkLive.exe. Keep the whole folder together: the exe needs the data folder and the .dll files beside it. To move it to another PC, copy the whole folder.

It needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The first time, Windows may say it protected your PC because the app is from an unknown publisher: choose More info → Run anyway.

The window opens full-screen and is called PatchWork Live.

Two views: the stage and the show pages

On a tablet you turn the tablet to move between the stage (sideways) and the show's pages (upright). A computer window stays wide, so there are two buttons instead:

  • Show pages (top right of the stage) — the Show and Master pages, the same pages the tablet shows upright.
  • Stage (top right of the show pages) — back to the stage plot.

Anywhere the app says "turn the tablet sideways", on Windows read it as "press Stage".

On the Master page the stage sits on the left and the master patch on the right, both visible at once. Patch and Plot fold open and shut, so you can give either the whole window.

Mouse

  • Click is a tap, and drag moves things, exactly as on the tablet. A piece and its person move together.
  • Mouse wheel zooms the stage about the pointer; the arrows button on the right (Fit) puts the whole stage back.
  • Right-click does what pressing and holding does on the tablet, in an act's piece picker (a position's sheet, a piece's own choices, a moved box back).
  • The stage starts LOCKED (bottom right) so a stray drag does nothing — click it to unlock, click again to lock. Unlocked, clicking pieces selects them and an orange bar shows Rotate all · Duplicate all · Delete all.

Keyboard

  • Delete (or Backspace) takes the selected pieces off the stage, with an Undo message.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) redoes. They work on the stage; the Undo and Redo buttons in the top bar and on the toolbar always work too.
  • Typing in a box (a name, a number) is never interrupted by these keys.

Where your files are

Windows has no Download folder for the app the way an Android tablet does, so:

  • Plots are kept in Documents\plots — one file each, ending .stageplot.
  • The open show, your settings and the plot archive are kept by the app in its own data folder under your user profile (AppData). You don't need to touch them.
  • Saving a show to a file (⋮ → Show → More saving → Save as… or Save as zip) uses the normal Windows Save window — you choose the folder. The same goes for opening: ⋮ → Show → Open show… → Other file… opens the Windows Open window, which lists shows (.stageshow), plots (.stageplot) and zips.

Moving a show between the PC and the tablet

  1. On the one you built it on: ⋮ → Show → More saving → Save as zip. The zip holds the show, every act's plot and the schedule.
  2. Get the zip across (a USB cable, a cloud drive, an email to yourself).
  3. On the other: ⋮ → Show → Open show… and pick the zip. On the tablet, the zip goes in the Download/PatchWork Live folder or you pick it with Other file….
Do a Save as zip before you close a long session on the PC. It is also your backup.

What works the same

The show pages (days, stages, running order, changeovers), the master patch and master plot, band plots and the plot editor, the schedule reader (from a file, pasted text, a PDF or a spreadsheet), the plot archive and Past bands, printing, and Help with this manual.

What isn't on Windows

  • Scanning a QR with the camera, and photo-based reading — use a file or pasted text.
  • The on-device rider reader (the one that reads a photo or PDF on the tablet); riders and schedules are read from files, text and spreadsheets.
  • Reading a schedule straight from a website's page, and the shake gesture for feedback — use Help → Send feedback.
  • Purchases, Google sign-in and push notices. The crew's phone app, 2ndRodeo, is for phones.

If something is wrong

  • The window opens off-screen or too big: press the Windows key + Up to maximise it, or drag it by the title bar.
  • A shortcut does nothing: click the stage once so the app has the keyboard, and check no text box is open.
  • Anything else: Help → Send feedback sends a note and a picture of the screen to the app's maker.
