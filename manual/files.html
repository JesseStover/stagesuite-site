Files, cloud and sharing

Plots

Plots save as you work, one file each in Download/PatchWork Live/Plots; My plots is that folder. A plot someone sends opens with Import: if it's one of yours it updates — when you both changed it you choose Use theirs or Keep mine.

Shows

⋮ → Show: New show (Same venue keeps the house — master patch, house gear; New venue starts empty), Open show…, Save show. Save show as zip holds every act's plot, the schedule and the bands' files — the best way to move a show. Print: the Show sheet (running order, each changeover's jobs, box sheets), Show pack, Backline needs.

The cloud

Settings → You → Cloud: sign in with Google. ⋮ → Show → Cloud → Put this show in the cloud: every save goes up by itself, and your other devices open it with Open from the cloud…. Invite people by their crew line's Google email with their jobs — each job changes only its own part (Patch the master patch, Monitors the mixes…), the rest is look only. Someone else's change shows as a line under the top bar with Undo.

Backups and moving

Settings → Files & moving: Back up (every plot, the show and the house in one file), Move to another tablet…, and Files from before… (what an old install or another tablet left in Download/PatchWork Live). Your files stay if you reinstall.
