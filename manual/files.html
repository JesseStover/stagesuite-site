Files, cloud and sharing

Plots

Plots save as you work, one file each in Download/PatchWork Live/Plots; My plots is that folder. A plot someone sends opens with Import: if it's one of yours it updates — when you both changed it you choose Use theirs or Keep mine.

Shows

⋮ → Show: New show (Same venue keeps the house — master patch and plot, house backline, desks; New venue starts empty), Shows… (every saved show: Open, Rename, Duplicate, Share the file, Delete; Other file… opens a show or a promoter's zip from anywhere), Save show, Advance, Past bands. More saving → Save show as zip holds every act's plot, the schedule and the bands' files (written to Download/PatchWork Live/Shows) — the best way to move a show; Share show sends that zip. Print: Show sheet, Show pack, Backline needs.

The cloud

Cloud shows are switched off in this version. They return in a later one; this is how they work.

Settings → You → Cloud: sign in with Google. ⋮ → Show → Cloud → Put this show in the cloud: every save goes up by itself, and your other devices open it with Open from the cloud…. Invite people by their crew line's Google email with their jobs — each job changes only its own part (Patch the master patch, Monitors the mixes…), the rest is look only. Someone else's change arrives in Messages and shows a line with Undo their change.

Messages

The app no longer pops a line over the screen. Every line it would have said goes to a list: tap the envelope beside Undo (a yellow number counts the new ones), or Options → Messages. The list shows the newest first, with its time, where it came from and the details; a warning is bold. It holds the last 100 and is cleared when the app closes. Only a failed save, or a line with its own button, still shows on screen.

Backups and moving

Settings → Files & moving: Back up (every plot, the show and the house, into Download/PatchWork Live/Backups), Move to another tablet… (the same in one file to send), Files from before… (what an old install or another tablet left) and Start over on this tablet… (clears everything here — back up first). Your files stay if you reinstall.
