Reading riders

Bands send riders as PDFs, photos, spreadsheets or a few lines in an email. PatchWork Live reads them into a plot you check — with your own AI key, or free on the tablet.

A file

On the Show page tap a band's Plot or List tile. The window's corner button is always the next step: Add file, then Read, then Compare. Or use Add act → From a file. Options → Plot → Read an input list… (on the stage) makes a new plot from a photo or PDF on the tablet.

Each file, and each page of a PDF, is marked Stage plot, Input list, Backline or Other (Not sure when it could not tell): tap a mark to change it. Other pages are not read; a Backline page is read only by the tablet's reader. Turn straightens a page that came sideways. The tablet's reader reads counts ("3 vocal mics"), lists in columns, numbers on the band's drawing, amp models and the backline page.

Notes typed into an act

A rider pasted into an act's Notes is read by Read every band (the checklist lists it as "a rider typed in the notes"), the same way as a file. It reads sentences too — "Fiddle (mic on a short boom), bouzouki (DI), flute (mic)", "Bass: DI and a mic on the cab" (two inputs), "vocals: fiddle player and bouzouki player" (two vocals).

What it gets wrong

Clean lists read well; a page that mixes a drawing, a list and notes is read partly wrong. A read plot is not checked until you say so: the band's row has an orange !, and the stage shows not checked. Tap it (or the window's Compare) for the Check page: their page on the left (on top when upright) and the read on the right, in Plot and List views. Differences lists what does not match; a tap changes the plot to what they sent. What they sent is every page's mark. Looks right takes the orange mark off; Print view and Open the plot are at the bottom. The House switch: off, the band's window shows what they sent; on, the band is on your stage and a change is yours alone.

With your own AI

Save a Claude or OpenAI key in Settings → Your AI (a few cents a rider on your own account; the key stays on the tablet; Pro). Then a band's Read sends its marked pages to that AI and says what it cost. Without a key, Read opens the copy-and-paste window for your own AI app.

The round sparkle button at the end of the Show page's top line (or Settings → Read every band) opens a checklist of every band with a file or typed rider and no plot. Untick any, see the count and a rough cost, then Read n with Claude. With no key the tablet reads them, one Check page each.

Reading again

Tap a read band's name for its box: Read again (asks the price first), Read on this tablet (free, shows the page marks first) and Put back as they sent (lays their read on the house again and drops what you changed by hand; Undo brings it back). In the band's window Files adds or takes off a file.
